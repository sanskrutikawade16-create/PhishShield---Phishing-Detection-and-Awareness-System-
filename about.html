About PhishShield

Problem

Phishing uses social engineering to manipulate people into clicking links, sharing information or taking unsafe actions.

Objective

Demonstrate common phishing warning signs in a safe environment and teach practical countermeasures.

Ethics & Safety

This prototype does not collect credentials or personal information. For classroom testing, obtain informed consent before sharing the simulation with participants. Participation should be voluntary.

Technology

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and GitHub Pages.
